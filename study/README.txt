WAUTRACE GUIDED VISIT

The application opens in free exploration mode. Select Guided visit to open the study journey.

The configured instrument is the external Google Form:
https://forms.gle/vHHtfYTKqShZF1zc8

collection_enabled is false. The application does not collect or submit questionnaire responses. With this HTTPS questionnaire URL configured, the end of the tasks shows the external questionnaire link and a completion button; it does not show a second built-in questionnaire or create a response download. Clicking the completion button only ends the visit and does not verify a Google Form submission.

Lecturer/institutional study approval status remains pending in the supplied configuration. Record the actual approval arrangements separately before collecting research observations. The existence of the questionnaire link is not an approval statement.

The journey contains adult eligibility and voluntary participation confirmation, one prior-familiarity question, six neutral tasks and a completion screen. Tasks describe goals and ask participants to use their own wording. Marking a task attempted or skipped is not evidence of success. Familiarity describes the sample; it is not a learning pre-test. Local task and familiarity choices are not automatically passed to Google Forms.

The contact is Moustafa Hassan via the channel used for the study invitation. No unprovided email address or telephone number is assumed. The researcher may replace researcher_contact with a confirmed direct contact address.

Chat and nickname remain in the application session and are not sent to the questionnaire. The external form has its own collection and data handling arrangements. Withdraw and clear removes in-session study choices; it cannot remove an already submitted external form response. No automated submission or inspection of form responses is performed by this application.

cuq_adapted_instrument.json supplies the authorised 16 adapted CUQ items in order. Compare the external form with that file before participant use; the external form content has not been independently verified in this package. With no questionnaire URL configured, the built-in preview instrument remains available for local demonstration only under the pending approval configuration.

For analysis, positive items are the odd-numbered items and negative items are the even-numbered items. The CUQ score is 100 × [sum(odd response − 1) + sum(5 − even response)] / 64. The wording is adapted and its psychometric validation has not been established. Use the research plan for interpretation and source attribution.
