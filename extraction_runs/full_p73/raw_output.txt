## Ambiguities and scope notes

- C03–C04: The wording “with a head and dented/concaved” does not clearly specify what is dented or concaved. The affected component is unspecified.
- C11–C13: “It” and “its” are contextually associated with Wau Kikik/Wau Kaca, but the exact grammatical antecedent is not fully explicit. The passage supports the alternate name and the powdered-glass explanation without establishing a separate entity distinct from Wau Kikik.
- C14–C16: The passage describes an intended function during competition. It does not establish that the opponent’s wau is necessarily defeated in every contact.
- C19–C20: These are explicit negative claims about Wau Kikik. They should not be converted into positive claims or generalized to all traditional wau.
- C21: This is a comparison relation, not an identity relation. The passage does not state that Wau Kikik is identical to other traditional wau.
- C24–C25: “Like the shape of a leaf” describes a resemblance. It does not establish that the kite is literally a leaf or that every traditional Malay wau has this feature.
- Source year: 2002 is supplied as publication metadata; the passage does not independently state that year.
- Current status: “Today” is preserved as the source’s temporal expression. The passage alone does not establish whether the official status remains true in 2026.
